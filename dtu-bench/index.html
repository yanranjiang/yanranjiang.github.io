What does “boundary” mean?

The saved moment where the experiment branches. It is not a reset to the beginning of the task.

  1. 1. Execute the old taskThe original instruction guides the robot up to a recorded action.
  2. 2. Save & branchClear pending actions. Apply the branch instruction before fresh inference.
  3. 3. Compare continuationsLeft and right follow the displayed instructions for an equal recorded budget.
What if the video begins at the boundary?

Some clips omit pre-update footage because it could not be verified. The orange pause is a display aid, not simulated time. Neutral-posture cases add controller preparation before continuation; layout probes intentionally change the physical state.
